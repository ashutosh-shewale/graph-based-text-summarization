Heavy rain caused the Mill River to flood the town of Eastbrook on Tuesday night.
The Mill River flooded Eastbrook on Tuesday night after hours of heavy rain.
Water levels rose more than two metres above normal near the old stone bridge.
Emergency crews evacuated about three hundred residents from homes along the river.
Around three hundred residents living along the river were evacuated by emergency crews.
The town council opened the school gym as a temporary shelter for evacuated families.
Volunteers brought food, blankets and dry clothes to the shelter throughout the night.
No injuries were reported, but several roads and two shops were badly damaged.
Officials said the damage to roads and shops would take weeks to repair.
Forecasters expect the rain to stop by Thursday, allowing the river to fall slowly.
The council will meet on Friday to discuss better flood defences for Eastbrook.
